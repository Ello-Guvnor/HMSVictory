Orders

  1. Click a square on the Royal Navy Fleet board to place the ship marked Next. Ships run right or down from that square.
  2. Press Rotate (or R) to switch between horizontal and vertical. While pointing at your board with the mouse, ←/→ set horizontal and ↑/↓ set vertical.
  3. Or press Random placement. Start battle unlocks once all five ships are placed.
  4. Keyboard only: Tab to your board, move with the arrow keys, place with Enter, rotate with R.
